# Contact

Write to <help@labmill.org>.

Please tell us:

- your computer (macOS, Windows or Linux) and the Labmill version (`labmill version`);
- what you asked Labmill to do, and what happened;
- the report from **Copy diagnostics** (desktop app, **Settings > About and diagnostics**). It holds no passwords, keys, sign-in codes or user names.

Do not send passwords, API keys or private keys by email.

The privacy policy is at <https://labmill.org/privacy/>. Releases and downloads are at <https://github.com/labmill/labmill-client/releases>.
