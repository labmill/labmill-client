API¶

The API is at https://app.labmill.org/api/. It answers in JSON. There are three kinds of access:

  • public: no sign-in;
  • program token: the header Authorization: Bearer lmt_... with a token from Settings (see Your account);
  • browser session: only from the signed-in web app.

Limit: 10 requests a second for each address.

Public routes¶

Route Answer
GET /api/systems?q= Listed HPC systems with institution, scheduler, fact count, partitions, GPUs and the date of the newest fact.
GET /api/systems/undocumented TOP500 systems without public facts in Labmill.
GET /api/institutions/{id} One institution with its listed systems.
GET /api/facts Public facts with system, institution and scope.
GET /api/stats Counts of institutions, systems and facts.
GET /api/auth/providers The sign-in providers that are on.

Program token routes¶

Route Use
GET /api/me The signed-in person: id, role, display name, and whether the account contributes data.
GET /api/institutions?q= Institution search, 10 results at most. The query needs 2 characters or more.
GET /api/systems/resolve?host=&name=&domain= Find the system of a cluster from its login host, cluster name or site domain.
POST /api/jobs Send a job record.
GET /api/jobs, GET /api/jobs/{id} Your jobs.
POST /api/client-events Problem reports of the client: 1 to 50 events, 256 KB, at most 6 calls a minute.
POST /api/help, GET /api/messages Ask your research computing team for help and read the answers.
POST /api/recipes, GET /api/recipes, GET /api/recipes/{id}, DELETE /api/recipes/{id} Save, list, read and delete your run recipes.
POST /api/test-runs Results of a client test run.
GET /api/knowledge/manifest, GET /api/knowledge/bundle The HPC skills of the client. They need a subscription or the trial.

All other routes (consent, billing, teams, tokens, account) need the browser session. Every person sees only their own records.
