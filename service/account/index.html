Your account¶

Sign in¶

Go to app.labmill.org and sign in with Google, GitHub, LinkedIn or ORCID. Labmill makes an account at your first sign-in.

  • Google, GitHub and LinkedIn give Labmill a verified email address. When you later sign in with another of these providers and the same email address, you get the same account.
  • ORCID gives Labmill no email address. An account made with ORCID has no verified email and does not join an account made with another sign-in.

To sign out on this computer, use the menu at the top right. To sign out on all devices, open Settings.

Connect the client¶

The client uses a program token to send job records and problem reports to your account.

  1. Open Settings and make a program token. Give it a name, for example the name of your computer.
  2. Copy the token. The web app shows it only once.
  3. In the terminal app, run labmill login and paste the token. In the desktop app, open Settings, then Labmill account.

A program token can only call the routes that the client needs (see API). It cannot change your settings, your consent or your subscription. You can have at most 10 tokens. Revoke a token in Settings when you do not use that computer any more.

What Labmill keeps¶

  • Your name, email address and the sign-in provider.
  • The job records that the client sends: the scheduler facts (state, resources, times) and, when you share a job, its script and the last lines of its output.
  • The problem reports of the client.

Mark a job private in the client to send only the scheduler facts. With the contribution setting off, your whole account is private: the client then sends only scheduler facts.

To delete your account and its data, open Settings and use Delete account. Details: Privacy and Terms.
